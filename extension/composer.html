      Privacy Policy

      What stays on your device

      CkretGuard scans prompts locally. Settings, custom field names, and activity totals stay in this browser. When you choose Send for a prompt with detected secrets, History saves the complete unmasked prompt, detected count, chat site, conversation link when available, and time.

      What History includes

      Only unmasked prompts submitted through CkretGuard’s Send choice are saved. Masked prompts, ordinary messages, edited drafts, and cancelled sends are not saved. History begins with this update; earlier prompts cannot be recovered. An entry records a send attempt, not confirmation that the chat service received it.

      Storage and deletion

      History is stored in this extension’s local database. It is not synced or uploaded by CkretGuard, and CkretGuard does not encrypt it. Entries stay until you delete them, clear extension data, or uninstall the extension, subject to browser storage limits. You can delete one entry or clear History. Deleting History does not change activity totals or remove messages already shared with a chat service.

      Coverage

      CkretGuard has no server or remote analytics. The chat website can read text entered into its own page before you send it. Protection covers recognized ChatGPT and Claude text inputs and send controls. Files, images, voice, other apps, and existing conversations are outside its scanning coverage. Detection can miss secrets.
